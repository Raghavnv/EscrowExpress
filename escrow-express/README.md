# Escrow Express: Cash to Close

An interactive React + Phaser 3 mini-game built for **Nest Navigate** to help first-time homebuyers master **Closing Costs, Inspection Contingencies, Rate Lock Delays, and Prepaid Escrow Reserves**.

## Game Concept
* **The Concept**: Teaches first-time homebuyers the financial reality of **Cash to Close** and **Escrow Reserves**. Many buyers save their exact down payment ($30,000–$60,000) and are caught off guard when lenders require an additional 2%–5% in liquid cash at the closing table for origination fees, prepaid property taxes, and homeowner's insurance.
* **The Mechanic**: After registering a local buyer profile and choosing a loan vehicle (*Conventional Cruiser* vs. *FHA Sprinter*), players pilot a moving truck along the highway to the County Title Office. Liquid cash serves as fuel, while an attached **Escrow Trailer** must be filled before crossing the final bridge. Along the route, players make tactical decisions at three real-world checkpoints:
  1. **Home Inspection Roadblock**: Negotiate seller credits, pay out-of-pocket, or risk waiving the inspection.
  2. **Underwriting Paperwork Gridlock**: Actively cut the truck's engine during processing delays to freeze rate-lock extension fees ($150/sec bleed).
  3. **The Escrow Weigh Station**: Transfer cash into the escrow lockbox to prepay 6 months of taxes and 12 months of hazard insurance.
* **The Learning Outcome**: Players understand why down payments aren't the only upfront cost, how seller credits protect liquid cash, why rate locks matter during delays, and how to avoid becoming "house poor" on move-in day.
* **Why It's Fun**: It transforms intimidating mortgage disclosures into a tactile 2D driving survival game with real-time particle effects, countdown tension, and an itemized **Closing Disclosure** scorecard.
